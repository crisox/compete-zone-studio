---
name: Omni prompt leakage
description: Why technical branding specifications must be kept out of Omni's creative input.
---

Treat every literal palette code sent to Gemini Omni as potential rendered copy, not just production metadata. Describe colors in ordinary words instead.

**Why:** Actual CompeteZone videos displayed palette codes both in title cards and on flags within sports scenes. A restriction aimed only at foreground titles would miss environmental signage.

**How to apply:** Keep codes out of all prompt sources, including brand tone and pasted user guidelines. Separate visual/audio direction from advertising copy, and apply text restrictions to background objects too. Do not send the official logo to be redrawn; compose the saved artwork afterward.
